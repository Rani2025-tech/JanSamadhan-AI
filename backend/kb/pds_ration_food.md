# Food Supplies & Consumer Welfare (PDS / Ration) FAQ

## Q1: Fair Price Shop (dealer) refuses to disburse monthly grain quota.
Under NFSA / SFSA regulations, dealers cannot deny mandated rice/wheat allocations to active ration card holders. Grievance is auto-routed to the Inspector of Supplies. Inspections are conducted within 5 days.

## Q2: Biometric Aadhaar e-KYC or fingerprint failure at e-POS machine.
Dealers are equipped with OTP-based and Iris authentication alternatives. In case of chronic biometric failure (elderly citizens / manual laborers), a nominee delivery authorization can be registered.

# Public Health & Family Welfare FAQ

## Q1: Absence of duty doctor or essential medicine in PHC/CHC.
Grievance Category: "Public Health". Auto-routed to the Chief District Medical Officer (CDMO). Investigations on doctor absenteeism are initiated within 48 hours.

## Q2: Anti-rabies or anti-venom injection stock out.
Critical urgency ticket. CDMO drug store transfers stock immediately from the nearest district headquarters hospital within 12 hours.
