# Public Works Department (PWD) - Roads, Bridges & Drainage FAQ

## Q1: How to report dangerous potholes or washed-out road stretches?
Report via Category "Road Infrastructure" under PWD. Providing a photo or exact landmark (e.g., Near Bus Stand, SH/NH junction) triggers automated assignment to the Sub-Divisional Engineer. Deep potholes causing accident hazards receive High urgency.

## Q2: Who is responsible for colony internal roads versus state highways?
- Internal colony and municipal ward roads are maintained by the local Municipal Corporation / Municipality.
- Major district roads (MDR) and state highways (SH) are maintained by the State PWD (Roads & Building) Division.
- JanSamadhan AI automatically maps the responsible authority from the address provided.

## Q3: What is the resolution SLA for road damage?
- Cold-mix bitumen pothole patch repair: 3 to 5 days
- Broken bridge guardrail / cave-in: 24 to 48 hours (temporary barricading within 4 hours)
- Full resurfacing / blacktopping: Scheduled in annual maintenance calendar with immediate leveling
