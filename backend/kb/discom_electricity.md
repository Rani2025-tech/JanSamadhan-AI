# DISCOMs (TPCODL, TPWODL, TPSODL, TPNODL) - Electricity FAQ

## Q1: How to report sparking wires, fallen poles, or transformer burns?
This is classified as Critical Urgency under DISCOM. The automated routing triggers an emergency SMS to the local electrical junior engineer and lineman. Response time for power disconnection of exposed live wire is under 2 hours. Replacement of a burnt distribution transformer is mandated within 24 hours in urban areas and 48 hours in rural areas.

## Q2: How to report persistent low voltage or phase outage?
Report under Category "Electricity". Low voltage issues usually require tap changer adjustment or phase load balancing at the local substation. Resolution timeline is 3 business days.

## Q3: Streetlights not functioning on our street - whom to contact?
If streetlights are dark, file a complaint on JanSamadhan under "Electricity" or "Municipal Corporation". Energy Management teams replace burnt LED fixtures and timer switches within 48 to 72 hours.
