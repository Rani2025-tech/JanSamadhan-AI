# Municipal Corporation & Sanitation Services FAQ

## Q1: Doorstep garbage collection vehicle is not visiting our area.
Grievance category: "Sanitation". The Sanitary Inspector of the municipal ward is alerted. Door-to-door collection schedule is resumed within 24 hours.

## Q2: Drains are overflowing or blocked with plastic silt.
Clogged open storm drains cause waterlogging and vector-borne diseases. Silt extraction and high-pressure suction clearing is assigned with a 48-hour SLA.

## Q3: Dead animal removal or urgent fogging requirement.
Dead animal removal carcass vans operate within 4 to 6 hours of ticket registration. Mosquito fogging is deployed within 48 hours in wards with fever outbreaks.
