# National Public Service Guidelines & Citizen Charters (All India)

## Q1: How do I apply for a new drinking water connection or report contaminated water across India?
Under the Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) and AMRUT urban guidelines, every household has a right to potable piped water supply. For contamination or pipeline breaks, report the issue immediately with your city/ward or district water supply department. Urban civic bodies must resolve pipeline leaks within 48 to 72 hours, and emergency water tankers must be dispatched to affected areas within 24 hours.

## Q2: What are my rights regarding unexpected power cuts, high electricity bills, and damaged transformers?
Under the Electricity Act (2003) and the National Electricity (Rights of Consumers) Rules:
1. Urban areas are entitled to 24x7 continuous power supply; rural areas must receive minimum 20 hours daily.
2. Burnt transformers must be replaced within 24 hours in urban areas and 48 hours in rural areas.
3. Inaccurate or inflated bills must be inspected and revised within 7 working days by the local DISCOM / electricity board.

## Q3: Can I collect food grains on my ration card in any state under One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)?
Yes. Under the One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC) initiative by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, NFSA beneficiaries can collect their subsidized food grains from any Fair Price Shop (e-PoS enabled) anywhere in India using biometric (Aadhaar) authentication. No citizen can be denied their quota for being outside their home state.

## Q4: What are the redressal timelines for civic grievances like potholes, streetlights, and garbage dumps?
Under the citizen charter of Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) and Municipal Corporations nationwide:
1. Non-functioning street lights: Must be repaired within 48 hours of reporting.
2. Dangerous potholes on arterial and PWD roads: Temporary asphalt leveling within 48-72 hours.
3. Overflowing garbage bins and drain blocks: Sanitary teams must clear dumps within 24 hours of civic complaint registration.
