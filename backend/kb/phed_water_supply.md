# Public Health Engineering Department (PHED) & Water Corporation FAQ

## Q1: How do I report a broken drinking water pipeline or street water leakage?
You can file a grievance under Category "Water Supply" with the PHED department on JanSamadhan. Mention the ward number, street landmark, and duration of leakage. The PHED maintenance wing deploys a repair team within 24 hours. The standard SLA for pipeline repair is 3 to 7 days.

## Q2: What should I do if tap water is contaminated, muddy, or foul-smelling?
Contaminated water is treated as a Critical Urgency grievance. Immediate water sampling and pipeline flushing are mandated under the Odisha Drink From Tap Mission. Water tanker supply is dispatched within 6 hours to affected blocks while pipe repair takes place.

## Q3: How do I request an emergency water tanker in my locality?
If pipe supply is disrupted for more than 24 hours, citizens can request emergency water tankers via JanSamadhan or by calling the toll-free helpline 1800-123-4567. Tankers are supplied free of charge by the municipal water division.

## Q4: What is the maximum resolution time (SLA) for water supply issues?
- Low pressure / valve regulation: 4 days
- Minor pipeline burst / leak: 48 hours
- Contamination alert: 24 hours (Critical priority)
- New connection / meter rectification: 7 days
